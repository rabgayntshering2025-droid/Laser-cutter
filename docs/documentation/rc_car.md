# My RC Car

I will build my 4-wheel robot car by assembling a simple frame, mounting four DC motors, and connecting an Arduino board in the middle to act as the main brain. I will place an ultrasonic sensor at the front to act as the eyes, an L293D driver chip to control the wheels, a warning buzzer, and a battery pack for power. After wiring all the parts together, I will program the car to drive forward until an obstacle gets closer than 25 centimeters, at which point it will sound the alarm, back up, and turn to find an open path.  How I Will Make It Step-by-StepAssemble the Base: I will mount four DC motors and wheels onto a car frame so it can move using 4-wheel drive.  Mount the Brain: I will place the Arduino board in the center to receive sensor readings and run the movement logic.  Set Up Motor Control: I will wire an L293D motor driver chip between the Arduino and motors to send power to the left and right wheels.  Attach the Eyes: I will place the HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor on the front bumper to measure distance using sound waves.  Connect the Warning Alarm: I will wire a small piezo buzzer to sound a continuous beep whenever an object gets too close.  Hook Up Power and Code: I will connect a battery pack to run the motors, then upload the program to make the car automatically stop, buzz, and turn away from obstacles.
