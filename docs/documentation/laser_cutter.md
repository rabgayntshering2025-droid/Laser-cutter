# Laser Cutter

A laser cutter is a computer-controlled (CNC) machine that uses a tightly focused, high-powered beam of light to precisely cut, engrave, or etch materials.

## How to design and use Inkscape

* *Set up your page size: Open Inkscape and press Shift + Ctrl + D. Change Display units to mm, then type in the width and height of your wood or acrylic*

* *Draw the shape you want to cut out: Pick the Rectangle or Circle tool on the left toolbar and draw your outer border (for example, a 60 mm × 30 mm keychain tag). If you need a hole, draw a small 4 mm circle inside it.*

* *Make cut lines and ultra-thin: Select your cut shapes and press Shift + Ctrl + F to open the Fill and Stroke panel:*

* *Fill tab: Click the X (No paint) so the inside is hollow.*

* *Stroke paint tab: Set the Stroke paint to Flat Color*

* *Stroke style tab: Change Width to Hairline. (Hairline tells the laser to cut all the way through).*

* *Add text or designs to engrave: Click the Text tool (A) on the left, type your text, and place it inside your shape. Or use pinterest or googole to find a design.*
